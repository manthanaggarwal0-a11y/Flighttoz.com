Frequently Asked Questions

Why don't you show prices on the website?+

Airline fares change many times a day, so a price on a website is often out of date. Send us your trip details and our team replies with current fares, taxes included, on WhatsApp or email.

Can I get my quote in Canadian dollars (CAD)?+

Yes. Choose USD, CAD or INR in the quote form and we will quote in that currency.

How do I book?+

Fill in the quote form and tap Send on WhatsApp or Send by Email. Your trip details are filled in for you, so just press send. Our team replies with options; once you choose, we share the payment details and issue your e-ticket.

Can I hold a fare without paying?+

Where the airline allows it, yes. Mention it in your request and we'll try to hold the fare for a limited time. Held fares are not guaranteed until the ticket is issued.

What does a quote include?+

One total price: the fare, taxes, airline fees, our service fee and any card fee. For trips starting in Canada, we show the total in CAD first. Fares can change until the ticket is issued.

What if I need to change or cancel?+

Message us with your booking reference. Changes and refunds follow the airline's fare rules. Read our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
