Frequently Asked Questions

Are the prices on the website final?+

No. Fares shown are indicative estimates. Airline prices change often, so our team confirms the exact price, taxes and availability on WhatsApp before you book.

Can I see prices in Canadian dollars (CAD)?+

Yes. Use the USD / CAD / INR switch in the search box (or at the top of the page on larger screens). All fares update instantly. Conversions are approximate; your final quote states the exact amount.

How do I book?+

Search your trip and tap Book Now on the option you like. WhatsApp opens with your trip details filled in. Our team confirms the fare, shares the payment details and issues your e-ticket.

Can I hold a fare without paying?+

Where the airline allows it, yes. Tap Hold Now and we'll try to hold the fare for a limited time. Held fares are not guaranteed until the ticket is issued.

How does the FirstFlight15 coupon work?+

Mention FirstFlight15 when you book your first trip with us. It's added to your WhatsApp message automatically, and our team applies the discount to eligible bookings. See our terms.

What if I need to change or cancel?+

Message us with your booking reference. Changes and refunds follow the airline's fare rules. Read our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
