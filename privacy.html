Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Flight to Z ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use flighttoz.com or contact us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, travel dates, routes and passenger details you share when you search, enquire or book.
  • Passenger details for ticketing: names as on passport, date of birth, passport or ID details where an airline requires them.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting provider may log your IP address and browser type for security and performance.

How we use it

  • To prepare quotes, hold and book flights, and send you tickets and updates.
  • To reply to your messages and provide customer support.
  • To meet legal, tax and airline requirements.

Sharing

We share passenger details only with airlines, consolidators and payment providers needed to complete your booking, or when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

When you message us on WhatsApp or by email, those services process your messages under their own privacy policies. This website does not load any third-party scripts, fonts, trackers or advertising.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use cookies. The website remembers your chosen currency (USD, CAD or INR) in your browser's local storage so it stays selected next time. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Search details and contact messages are not stored on the website; they are sent only when you choose to open WhatsApp or your email app.

Data retention

We keep booking records for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, subject to legal record-keeping requirements. Contact us using the details below.
